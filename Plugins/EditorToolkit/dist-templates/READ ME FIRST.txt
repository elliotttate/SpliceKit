Editor Toolkit for SpliceKit

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac with Final Cut Pro and SpliceKit already working.

TO INSTALL
1. Right-click "Install.command" and choose Open.
   (The first time, macOS asks if you're sure because it was downloaded from the internet. Click Open.
    If there's no Open button, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".)
2. A Terminal window lists what was installed. Press any key to close it.
3. Quit Final Cut Pro and open it again the way you normally start it with SpliceKit.

TO REMOVE
Right-click "Uninstall.command", choose Open, then restart Final Cut Pro.

What each plugin does: see the README.md inside each folder in "plugins", or
https://github.com/ishubhampratap/SpliceKit/tree/editor-toolkit/Plugins/EditorToolkit

Tested on Final Cut Pro 12.4, SpliceKit 3.3.9, Apple Silicon.
